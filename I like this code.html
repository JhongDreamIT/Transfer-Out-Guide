Option 1: Standard SEVIS Transfer

This is the standard, low-risk route (In-Status) for students who successfully finish their current term.

  • Attendance: Must maintain at least 80% attendance.
  • Academics: Must complete the semester, taking all tests and final exams.
  • Financials: Must pay the current semester's tuition in full (zero balance).
  • Timing: Must start classes at the new school within 5 months of the semester's end date.
  • Explain the Rules: Inform the student they must finish the semester, take all tests, and pay full tuition.
  • Collect Documents: Have the student bring an Acceptance Letter and Transfer-In Form from the new school.
  • Verify Compliance: At the end of the semester, confirm the student met the attendance, academic, and financial requirements.
  • Release SEVIS: Once verified, work with the DSO to schedule the SEVIS release date.

Option 2: Terminate & Reinstate

This route is for students who drop out mid-semester or fail to meet the Option 1 requirements. They will fall out of status.

  • 5-Month Rule: Must secure admission to a new school within 5 months of their SEVIS termination date.
  • USCIS Filing: The student must independently file Form I-539 with USCIS to ask for their F-1 status back.
  • Warn the Student: Clearly explain that dropping out means losing their F-1 status and work benefits. Reinstatement by USCIS is never guaranteed, and a denial means they must leave the U.S. immediately.
  • Terminate Record: The DSO terminates the student's SEVIS record for failing to complete the term.
  • Collect Documents: Instruct the student to get an Acceptance Letter and a Reinstatement I-20 from their new school.
  • Direct to USCIS: Tell the student they must file Form I-539 (along with their new I-20, acceptance letter, fees, and a letter of explanation) directly with USCIS.
